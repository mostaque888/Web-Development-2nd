The planets of our Solar System

The Solar System has eight planets that orbit the Sun. Each planet is unique in its size, temperature, surface and atmosphere. Some planets are rocky, like Earth and Mars, while others are gas giants, like Jupiter and Saturn. Learning about the planets helps us understand space, our place in the universe, and how different worlds exist beyond Earth.

Mercury

The closest planet to the Sun and one of the smallest planets.

Venus

A rocky planet with thick clouds and extreme heat.

Earth

Our home planet and the only world known to support life.

Mars

A dusty red planet linked with rovers, volcanoes and the search for past water.

Read the Mars page

Jupiter

The largest planet in the Solar System, famous for its giant storms.

Read the Jupiter page

Saturn

Known for its bright rings made from ice and rock.

Uranus

An icy giant with a tilted rotation that makes it unusual.

Neptune

A cold blue world with powerful winds and storms.
