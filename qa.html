Questions and Answers

Click the button to show answers about space.

Question 1: What is the Milky Way?

The Milky Way is the galaxy that contains our Solar System.

Question 2: Which planet is called the red planet?

Mars is called the red planet.

Question 3: Which planet has famous rings?

Saturn is famous for its rings.

Question 4: What is the largest planet?

Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System.

Question 5: What is a galaxy?

A galaxy is a huge system of stars, gas, dust and planets held together by gravity.

Question 6: Is the Solar System inside the Milky Way?

Yes, the Solar System is inside the Milky Way galaxy.

Question 7: Why does Mars look red?

Mars looks red because its surface contains iron-rich dust that gives it a rusty colour.

Question 8: How many planets are in our Solar System?

There are eight planets in our Solar System.

Question 9: Which planet do we live on?

We live on Earth.

Question 10: Which planet is closest to the Sun?

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.

Question 11: Which planet is known for strong winds?

Neptune is known for very strong winds and storms.

Question 12: Does Mars have robots on it?

Yes, Mars has been explored by robotic rovers sent by space agencies.

Question 13: What are planets?

Planets are large objects in space that orbit a star, such as the Sun.

Question 14: Which planet is the hottest?

Venus is the hottest planet in our Solar System.

Question 15: Can people live on Mars now?

No, people do not currently live on Mars because it does not have the right conditions for human life.
